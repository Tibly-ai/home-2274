What Affects Your City Hall Photography Price

  • How many locations you want documented. A ceremony-only shoot at the Marriage Bureau costs less than a full "subway-to-skyline" day that adds stops like Ralph's Coffee or The Met.
  • Time of day. Marriage Bureau ceremonies happen Monday–Friday between 8:30am and 3:45pm — booking earlier in that window often means shorter waits and softer light for photos.
  • Add-ons. A second shooter or a getting-ready segment before the ceremony adds to the base package.
  • Season. Spring (April–June) and fall (September–November) are the most requested seasons for outdoor stops around Central Park and the Upper East Side, which can affect availability and pricing.

The NYC Marriage License: What It Actually Costs

Before your City Hall ceremony, you'll need a marriage license:

  • Apply online or in person at the City Clerk's Office, main office at 141 Worth Street, Manhattan
  • Both parties must be present with valid photo ID
  • In Manhattan, a marriage license costs $35
  • There is a mandatory 24-hour waiting period after obtaining the license before you can marry
  • The license is valid for 60 days, so plan your elopement timeline accordingly

Pro tip: apply for your license a couple of days before your ceremony so the 24-hour window has already passed.
