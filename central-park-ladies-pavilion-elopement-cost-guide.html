What Affects Your Price

Several factors move the number up or down from the base range above:

  • Time of day. Early morning (7–9am) sessions mean quieter crowds and softer light; golden-hour sessions are in higher demand and may book faster.
  • Season. Spring (cherry blossoms) and fall (foliage) are peak-demand seasons in Central Park and the Upper East Side area, per our own seasonal guide.
  • Number of locations. A Ladies Pavilion-only session costs less than a package that also includes a walk to Bow Bridge, Bethesda Terrace, or a second neighborhood like DUMBO or the West Village.
  • Guest count. A true elopement (just the couple) is priced differently than a micro wedding with a small guest list.

Before booking your date, plan around these logistics:

  • Marriage license: Required before any NYC ceremony. Apply at the City Clerk's Office (141 Worth Street, Manhattan); cost is $35, with a mandatory 24-hour waiting period and 60-day validity window. (Full steps in our How to Elope in NYC guide.)
