What Affects Your Price

Several factors typically shift the final quote for a Hoboken or Jersey City loft wedding:

  • Coverage length — a 2-hour elopement session costs less than full-day loft wedding coverage.
  • Number of locations — a single rooftop ceremony vs. a day that moves between a Hoboken loft, the waterfront, and a Jersey City rooftop for skyline shots.
  • Season and time of day — golden-hour and early-morning sessions require different scheduling than a nighttime, twinkle-light-and-skyline shoot.
  • Travel across the Hudson — sessions that combine a Hoboken or Jersey City ceremony with a Manhattan or DUMBO backdrop may involve additional travel time.
  • Add-ons — engagement sessions, second shooters, or expedited galleries (if offered) affect the total.
